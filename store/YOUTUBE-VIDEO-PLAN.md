# Aurora Extract: YouTube video plan (about 5 minutes)

**Working title:** Free Chrome Extension: Export Facebook, Instagram & TikTok Data to Excel (Aurora Extract)
**Alt titles:** "Export Instagram Comments & Followers to CSV, Free" / "Check Your Instagram Profile Health in 30 Seconds, Free"
**Goal:** viewers install the free extension. The paid Studios get one soft mention at the end.
**Format:** screen recording with your voice. A face camera is optional. Record at 1920x1080.

## Before you record (setup)
1. Install Aurora Extract 0.5.0 from the Chrome Web Store (use the real store version, not a dev build).
2. Use a clean Chrome profile: no other extensions, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off. Zoom the browser to 110%.
3. Pick demo targets that are public and fine to show: your own business profile, or a big brand page. Do not show private people's names, emails or phone numbers. Blur or crop any personal data in the results table.
4. Sign in to Facebook, Instagram and TikTok in that profile (Extract uses what you can already see).
5. Do a dry run of every demo once, so you know the results appear within a few seconds. Cut waiting time in the edit.
6. Use a good microphone and a quiet room. Record the voiceover after the screen footage if you are nervous: it is easier.

## Say and don't say
- Say: free, public data only, runs in your browser, nothing is sent to us.
- Don't promise: income, followers, "undetectable", "no ban risk", or that it works 100% of the time. Platforms change their pages, so say "if something doesn't load, update the extension".
- Don't scrape or show private people's data in the video.
- Don't say the extension itself uses AI. The AI step happens on the website after you paste.

## Edit checklist
1. Cut dead waiting time. Speed up long runs 4x with a "sped up" label.
2. Add zoom-ins on the buttons you click.
3. Add chapter text on screen at each section.
4. Add background music at low volume, no copyrighted tracks.
5. Add captions (YouTube can auto-generate them, then check the spelling of "Aurora Extract").
6. End screen: 20 seconds, with a subscribe button and one more video.
