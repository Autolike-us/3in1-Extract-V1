Help & privacy

🔒 Your data never leaves your computer

Aurora Extract has no server. Everything you collect is stored in this browser and exported by you. We never see your account, cookies, messages or results.

👀 Only what you can already see

The extension reads pages and lists that your own logged-in account can open. Private profiles, private groups you haven't joined and anyone's hidden information stay hidden. Emails and phone numbers are collected only when a person or business has made them public (bio, About section, listing text, business contact button).

⚖️ Use it responsibly

You are responsible for following each platform's terms and the privacy and marketing laws that apply to you (GDPR, CAN-SPAM, PECR, CCPA and local rules). Don't spam people you extract; use contacts for legitimate, relevant outreach, and honour opt-outs.

👀 Keep the page tab in front

While Aurora Extract works, leave the Facebook, Instagram or TikTok tab visible (you can use a second window for this dashboard). Chrome pauses hidden tabs, so the site stops loading more posts. A small badge on the page shows progress and has a Stop button; if the tab goes to the background, Aurora waits and carries on when it is back.

🐢 Why it's paced

Gentle speed keeps pauses between steps so your account behaves like a normal user. If a platform asks you to confirm it's you or shows a warning, stop and take a break.
